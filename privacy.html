Privacy & Policy

Transparency regarding how your data is handled, how our tools operate, and our developer contribution agreements.

At PALMTOOLS, our mission is to provide powerful, accessible utilities for everyone. We believe in transparency regarding your data and the underlying technology powering our platform.

1. Data Processing: Local vs. Backend (AI)

The PALMTOOLS ecosystem consists of hybrid technologies. It is important to understand how different tools handle your files and data:

Client-Side Tools: Many of our utilities (such as basic PDF converters and QR generators) run 100% locally in your web browser. When using these specific tools, your files, images, and text never leave your device and are never uploaded to any external server.

Backend & AI Tools: Certain advanced tools on our platform leverage Artificial Intelligence and complex processing that cannot be handled by a browser alone. When using these AI-powered features, your required data is securely transmitted to the PALMTOOLS Group backend databases for processing. We do not use this data to train foundational AI models without consent, and temporary processing files are routinely purged from our servers to protect your privacy.

2. Tool Contributions & Acquisitions

We actively encourage developers to contribute to the PALMTOOLS ecosystem. By submitting a tool through our contribution portal, you agree to the following terms:

Free For All: PALMTOOLS operates on a free-to-use philosophy. If your tool is accepted and integrated into our platform, it must be provided free of charge to all end users. We do not support paywalled features for individual tools.

Acquisition Pricing: The "Expected Acquisition Price" requested during submission represents the one-time buyout fee the PALMTOOLS Group will pay to acquire the rights to host, modify, and distribute your tool. Once a price is agreed upon and paid, the tool becomes part of the PALMTOOLS suite.

3. Analytics and Tracking

We collect basic, anonymized analytics to understand which tools are most popular and to monitor the health of our backend infrastructure. We do not sell personal tracking data to third-party advertisers.
